Thamar ثمر — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Summary

Thamar does not collect, send or share any data. Everything you enter stays on your own phone.

What the app stores

Your accounts, certificates, interest rates, dates, reminders and notes are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. There is no server, no sign-up, no analytics and no advertising.

Face ID and passcode

Face ID is handled entirely by your phone's operating system; Thamar never receives your face data or your phone passcode. If you set a Thamar passcode, only a one-way hash of it is kept in your phone's secure keychain.

Importing from pictures, lists and PDFs

When you import certificates, Thamar can read only the pictures or PDF files you pick yourself. Thamar has no access to the rest of your photo library or files. The text is read on your phone by Apple's built-in text recognition. Pictures and files are never uploaded, and Thamar does not keep a copy of them; only the certificate details you confirm are saved. Thamar never uses the camera or microphone.

Notifications

Maturity reminders are scheduled locally on your phone. They are not sent through any server.

Backups

A backup is a file encrypted with a password you choose each time, saved wherever you decide (for example Files or iCloud Drive). The password is never stored. Thamar never uploads the file anywhere.

Contact

Questions about this policy or the app can be sent through the Thamar support page.

الخصوصية باختصار

لا يجمع ثمر أي بيانات ولا يرسلها ولا يشاركها. كل ما تدخله يبقى على هاتفك فقط، بدون خوادم أو تسجيل أو إعلانات. الصور وملفات PDF التي تختارها للاستيراد تُقرأ على هاتفك فقط ولا تُرفع ولا تُحفظ. يتم التعامل مع بصمة الوجه بالكامل من نظام الهاتف، والنسخ الاحتياطية ملفات مشفّرة تحفظها أنت حيث تشاء.

Confidentialité en bref

Thamar ne collecte, n'envoie et ne partage aucune donnée. Tout ce que vous saisissez reste sur votre téléphone : aucun serveur, aucune inscription, aucune publicité. Les images et PDF que vous choisissez pour l'import sont lus sur votre téléphone, jamais envoyés ni conservés. Face ID est entièrement géré par le système du téléphone et les sauvegardes sont des fichiers chiffrés que vous enregistrez où vous voulez.
