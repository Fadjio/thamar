Thamar ثمر — Support

Need help or found a problem?

Open a request here: Thamar support requests. Please describe what you did and what happened. Never include your real amounts, certificate numbers or backup password.

Common questions

Where is my data? Only on your phone. Thamar has no server and no account.

I changed phones. Make a backup in Thamar (Settings → Backup), save the file to Files or iCloud Drive, then restore it on the new phone with the same password.

I forgot my backup password. It is never stored, so the backup cannot be opened without it. Make a new backup on your phone.

Reminders don't arrive. Check that notifications for Thamar are on in the iPhone's Settings.

Privacy policy

الدعم

لأي مساعدة أو مشكلة، افتح طلبًا من صفحة طلبات الدعم. لا تكتب مبالغك الحقيقية أو أرقام الشهادات أو كلمة مرور النسخة الاحتياطية.

بياناتك موجودة على هاتفك فقط. لنقلها إلى هاتف جديد: اعمل نسخة احتياطية من الإعدادات واسترجعها على الهاتف الجديد بنفس كلمة المرور.
